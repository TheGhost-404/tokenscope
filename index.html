Red flag glossary

Plain-English meanings. A flag is a warning, not proof of fraud.

Honeypot

A token you can buy but not sell. Detection is a simulation and can be wrong.

Mint authority

The ability to create more tokens, which can dilute holders. Revoked means it cannot be used again.

Freeze authority

On Solana, lets a creator freeze token accounts so holders cannot move tokens.

Liquidity lock

Locked liquidity cannot be pulled by the creator until the lock ends. Having a pool is not the same as being locked.

Holder concentration

When a few wallets hold most supply, one sale can crash the price. Pools and contracts can look like whales.

Bundled buying

Several wallets buying in the same launch moment, sometimes funded by one source. Not analyzed in this version.

FAQ

Does a low score mean a token is safe?

No. It only means fewer risks were detected from the data available. Always check confidence and inconclusive items.

How does "Community vs scam" work?

It weighs the checks (honeypot, taxes, holders, LP locks, listings, age and more) into a position between scam-like and community-like. Hard red flags override it, young tokens are never called community-like, and unchecked items such as creator history are listed. It is a read of the evidence, not a guarantee.

Do I need to connect a wallet?

Never. This tool is read-only and never asks for a seed phrase or private key.

Where does the data come from?

DexScreener, GoPlus, Jupiter, CoinGecko and the public Solana RPC. Each check names its source.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is information only and cannot predict future behavior.
