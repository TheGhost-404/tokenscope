Safe by design

TOKENSCOPE only reads public data. Here is exactly what it checks, and what it will never do.

What we check

  • Honeypot and sellability
  • Buy and sell tax
  • Liquidity depth, markets and lock
  • Mint and freeze authority
  • Ownership and contract permissions
  • Holder concentration
  • Contract verification
  • Token age
  • Buy and sell pressure
  • Volume compared to liquidity
  • CoinGecko and Jupiter listings
  • Deployer holdings

What we never do

  • Never connects to your wallet
  • Never asks for your seed phrase or private key
  • Never asks you to sign or approve anything
  • Never needs an account or sign-in
  • Never charges you or sells anything
  • Never gives buy or sell advice
  • Never promises a token is safe

Each line above is a promise: if any site asks for these, it is not us.

The honest details: to fetch data, the token address you enter is sent to public data providers (DexScreener, GoPlus, GeckoTerminal, CoinGecko and Jupiter). Your recent scans stay in your own browser. The font loads from Google Fonts, and the live map loads from Bubblemaps only when you tap it. Linked wallet clusters are not analyzed by TOKENSCOPE itself; on Solana it only compares when the top holders first received the token.

FAQ

Does a low score mean a token is safe?

No. It only means fewer risks were detected from the data available. Always check confidence and inconclusive items.

Do I need to connect a wallet?

Never. This tool is read-only and never asks for a seed phrase or private key.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is information only and cannot predict future behavior.
